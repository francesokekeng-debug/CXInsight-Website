About this site

What is an investigation?

A question about one customer journey, followed through: what happens today, what the rules say, where it breaks and what a better version looks like. Every claim is sourced.

What do "In progress" and "Published" mean?

"In progress" means I'm still researching or writing it, so please don't quote it yet. "Published" means it's finished and sourced.

What are field notes?

Shorter pieces: what I'm learning, plus a monthly roundup of regulation and customer experience news with a short note on why each item matters.

Is a journey breaking somewhere you work?

I'm glad to talk about research, collaboration, roles and speaking.
