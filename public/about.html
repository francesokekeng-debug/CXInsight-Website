About

Hi, I'm Frances.

I'm a product and customer experience professional based in Glasgow.

I've spent more than ten years in customer experience, operations and product research across telecoms, travel and technology. That work taught me where customer journeys tend to break: cancellations, complaints, investigations and the moments when a customer just wants something sorted.

I now research how UK consumer protection rules shape those journeys, and I build products that make them easier. I'm the founder of CXInsight Ltd, where we're building AxeoMove: one line on what it does.

I hold a Global MBA from Glasgow Caledonian University, an MSc in Operations and Production Management, and a CSPO certification.
