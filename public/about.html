Hello, I'm Frances.

I'm a product and customer experience researcher based in Glasgow.

I've spent more than ten years in customer experience, operations and product research across telecoms, travel and technology. That work showed me where customer journeys tend to break: cancellations, complaints and the moments when someone just wants something sorted.

Now I investigate how UK consumer protection rules shape those journeys, and I build things that make them easier. I'm the founder of CXInsight Ltd.

The line in my logo comes from uli, the line art of Igbo women, where the line leads and empty space matters as much as what is drawn. It's how I like to work: one clear line of thought, and room to think.
